\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Diffusion language models.}
Discrete and masked diffusion objectives carry diffusion~\citep{ho2020ddpm} over to text
\citep{austin2021d3pm,lou2024sedd,sahoo2024mdlm,shi2024simplified,ou2025radd} and now support
instruction-following systems such as LLaDA and Dream
\citep{nie2025llada,zhu2025llada15,ye2025dream}, with commercial deployments following the same
recipe~\citep{labs2025mercury}. Because the order in which positions commit
affects quality and throughput \citep{arriola2025blockdiffusion,kim2025ordering,wu2026fastdllm,
wei2026slowfast,benhamu2025entropy}, and because a run can equally be stopped before its step
budget is spent~\citep{lee2026commitlocallyexitglobally}, accelerators treat the whole schedule
as a tunable degree of freedom. Shibboleth, by contrast, treats it as
an invariant and draws on masked-token reconstruction only to define a probe it can later
repeat.

\paragraph{Watermarking autoregressive text.}
Autoregressive watermarks bias keyed token subsets~\citep{kirchenbauer2023watermark} or couple
sampling to keyed randomness~\citep{aaronson2023gpt,fu2024gumbelsoft,
kuditipudi2024distortionfree}, with later work characterising their reliability and
undetectability~\citep{kirchenbauer2024reliability,zhao2024provable,christ2024undetectable}. Detectors of this family are inexpensive precisely because a
key--token hash needs no generator, yet that hash is defined over a resolved prefix and does not
transfer unchanged to a context in which most token positions are still masked.

\paragraph{Watermarking diffusion language models.}
DLM-specific methods restore a well-defined keyed partition by other means, whether by removing
prefix dependence~\citep{chen2025orderagnostic}, by averaging a red--green bias over unresolved
context~\citep{gloaguen2026watermarking}, by keyed sampling~\citep{bagchi2025discrete}, or by
exploiting the decoding order itself~\citep{wu2025dmark,raban2026lrdwm,hong2026dgmark}. We
compare against dgMARK, the strongest order-steering baseline with a zero-inference
detector. Shibboleth differs from all of them in where the evidence resides, since
dgMARK spends the DLM's freedom over commit order and reads the outcome off the text, whereas
Shibboleth spends nothing from the schedule and instead has the verifier rerun the exact checkpoint,
buying a statistic bound to model behaviour at the price of a detector that no longer runs on
text alone.

\paragraph{Detector statistics.}
Detector design has moved past the asymptotic $z$-test: exact tails (binomial for green
counts, Gamma for Gumbel sums) close the gap between nominal and empirical FPR that Gaussian
approximations leave open at small $\alpha$~\citep{fernandez2023bricks}, permutation tests give
non-asymptotic $p$-values for otherwise intractable alignment
statistics~\citep{kuditipudi2024distortionfree}, and windowed maxima must either pay an explicit
union-bound correction or be calibrated empirically~\citep{kirchenbauer2024reliability}. When
edits leave watermark evidence in only a fraction of the text, sum-based pooling is provably
dominated by statistics that concentrate on the surviving fraction~\citep{li2025editrobust}.
Shibboleth's binomial tail belongs to the exact-test family, and its block structure admits the
same sparse-signal hardening, which Section~\ref{sec:calibration} records as an open direction
rather than a measured result.

\paragraph{Detection that consults the model.}
Every DLM watermark above detects from the text and key alone, but detectors that pay more for
their evidence are not new. Kuditipudi et al.'s edit-distance detector costs quadratic
alignment under thousands of permutation resamples~\citep{kuditipudi2024distortionfree}; with
model access, the Neyman--Pearson score strictly dominates the key-only Gumbel
statistic~\citep{fernandez2023bricks}, and log-likelihood-ratio detection reads both the
watermarked and base conditionals~\citep{hu2024unbiased}. GaussMark places its key in a
Gaussian perturbation of the weights and verifies with one forward--backward pass of the
generator~\citep{block2025gaussmark}, and watermarks built to expose model theft assume the
verifier can query the suspect system~\citep{zhao2023invisible}. Unlike GaussMark, which ships
a perturbed checkpoint, Shibboleth leaves the released weights as they are and puts the key in
the questions asked of them, and one deployed model therefore serves every document key.
