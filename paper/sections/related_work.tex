\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Diffusion language models.}
Continuous diffusion~\citep{ho2020ddpm} was extended to discrete data through structured
corruption processes~\citep{austin2021d3pm}, score-ratio models, and masked-diffusion
objectives~\citep{lou2024sedd,sahoo2024mdlm,shi2024simplified,ou2025radd}. More recent
systems scale this approach to instruction-following language models, including LLaDA
\citep{nie2025llada,zhu2025llada15} and Dream~\citep{ye2025dream}. ReTrace relies on one
basic consequence of masked-token training: a completed sequence can be corrupted again
and scored under several visible-context patterns.

Practical DLM decoding is neither fully parallel nor order-invariant. Systems commonly
complete blocks from left to right and denoise positions within a block
\citep{arriola2025blockdiffusion}; the unmasking order can affect the resulting text
\citep{kim2025ordering}. Work on accelerated decoding likewise finds that committing a
token before sufficient context is available can harm coherence
\citep{wu2026fastdllm,wei2026slowfast,benhamu2025entropy}. ReTrace therefore preserves the
model's reference block and confidence schedule. It changes token selection only through
resampling after the model has produced a conditional distribution.

\paragraph{Watermarks for autoregressive models.}
The dominant family modifies token probabilities. \citet{kirchenbauer2023watermark} add a
logit bias to a keyed green subset of the vocabulary, and detection counts green tokens.
Later work studies the reliability, robustness, and statistical guarantees of this design
\citep{kirchenbauer2024reliability,zhao2023invisible,zhao2024provable}. Another family
couples sampling randomness to a keyed pseudorandom sequence, using Gumbel-max sampling
\citep{aaronson2023gpt,fu2024gumbelsoft} or constructions with distortion-freeness and
undetectability guarantees~\citep{kuditipudi2024distortionfree,christ2024undetectable}.
Watermarks can also be placed in model parameters~\citep{block2025gaussmark}. Surveys
describe the broader design space~\citep{petitcolas1999hiding,liang2026survey}, including
paraphrase attacks~\citep{krishna2023paraphrasing} and key extraction
\citep{jovanovic2024stealing}. These methods provide an important efficiency reference:
their text detectors do not need to execute the generator.

\paragraph{Watermarks beyond left-to-right generation.}
Autoregressive watermarks cannot always be transferred directly to DLMs because unresolved
positions do not have a fixed left-to-right prefix. \citet{chen2025orderagnostic} avoid
prefix dependence when biasing token selection. For DLMs specifically,
\citet{gloaguen2026watermarking} apply a red--green bias in expectation over unresolved
context, while \citet{bagchi2025discrete} use keyed Gumbel-max sampling during denoising.
Other constructions target order-agnostic or efficient watermarking
\citep{wu2025dmark,raban2026lrdwm}. dgMARK~\citep{hong2026dgmark} instead steers the
unmasking order toward positions whose preferred token satisfies a keyed parity condition.
We reproduce dgMARK locally because it is a strong DLM-specific baseline with a different
embedding channel and a zero-inference detector.

\paragraph{Positioning of ReTrace.}
ReTrace differs primarily at verification time. Its statistic is not available from token
identities and the key alone: the verifier must run the DLM under keyed corruptions and
compare the resulting likelihoods. This model-response signal may expose structure that a
token hash does not, but it also makes verification substantially more expensive and ties
the detector to a particular checkpoint. To our knowledge, the cited language-model
watermarks do not use the generator as a reconstruction oracle in this form.
