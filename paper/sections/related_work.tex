\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Diffusion language models.}
Discrete and masked diffusion objectives extend diffusion to text
\citep{austin2021d3pm,lou2024sedd,sahoo2024mdlm,shi2024simplified,ou2025radd} and now
support instruction-following systems such as LLaDA and Dream
\citep{nie2025llada,zhu2025llada15,ye2025dream}. Practical decoders complete blocks from
left to right and use confidence to select positions within a block
\citep{arriola2025blockdiffusion}; token order affects quality and efficiency
\citep{kim2025ordering,wu2026fastdllm,wei2026slowfast}. ReTrace preserves this reference
schedule and uses masked-token reconstruction only to define a replayable response.

\paragraph{Text watermarking.}
Autoregressive watermarks bias keyed token sets~\citep{kirchenbauer2023watermark} or couple
sampling to keyed randomness~\citep{aaronson2023gpt,fu2024gumbelsoft,
kuditipudi2024distortionfree}; subsequent work studies reliability, robustness, and
undetectability~\citep{kirchenbauer2024reliability,zhao2024provable,
christ2024undetectable}. These token-only detectors are inexpensive but do not transfer
unchanged to unresolved DLM contexts. DLM-specific methods instead remove prefix dependence
\citep{chen2025orderagnostic}, average a red--green bias over unresolved context
\citep{gloaguen2026watermarking}, use keyed sampling~\citep{bagchi2025discrete}, or exploit
decoding order~\citep{wu2025dmark,raban2026lrdwm,hong2026dgmark}. We reproduce dgMARK
locally because it offers a strong order-steering baseline with zero-inference detection.
ReTrace differs at verification: the detector must rerun the exact DLM checkpoint under
keyed corruptions. This supplies a model-response statistic but incurs substantial cost.

The distinction is operational rather than cosmetic. A left-to-right green-list method
reintroduces a prefix solely to make its key--token partition well defined; dgMARK instead
uses the DLM-specific freedom to choose which position is committed next. ReTrace leaves
both the block partition and confidence-based position choice untouched. Its carrier is a
counterfactual response of the model to visible context, so the selected positions may be
text- and model-dependent while the secret orientation remains independent. This separation
is what permits an exact conditional test after adaptive, model-driven carrier selection.
It also explains why ReTrace should be viewed as complementary to inexpensive token hashes:
the added semantic binding to a checkpoint is useful only when model-assisted verification
and exact replay are acceptable deployment costs.
