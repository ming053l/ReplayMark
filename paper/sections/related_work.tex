\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Diffusion language models.}
Continuous diffusion~\citep{ho2020ddpm} was carried to discrete data by structured
corruption processes~\citep{austin2021d3pm} and by score-ratio and masked-diffusion
formulations~\citep{lou2024sedd,sahoo2024mdlm,shi2024simplified,ou2025radd}. Instruction-following
models at language-model scale followed --- LLaDA and its successors~\citep{nie2025llada,zhu2025llada15},
Dream~\citep{ye2025dream} --- alongside industrial systems built on the same
paradigm~\citep{labs2025mercury}. The property we exploit is the training objective itself:
these models predict masked tokens from arbitrary surviving context, so they can be applied
to a \emph{finished} text and not only used to produce one.

Two further findings from this literature shape our design. First, practical dLLMs are
\emph{not} order-invariant even though an ideal conditional predictor would be, and the
choice of unmasking order measurably changes what is generated~\citep{kim2025ordering}.
Second, decoding is in practice semi-autoregressive: blocks are filled in sequence and
diffusion happens within a block~\citep{arriola2025blockdiffusion}, and work on accelerating
that loop shows that committing a confident token whose left context is still unresolved
damages coherence~\citep{wu2026fastdllm,wei2026slowfast,benhamu2025entropy}. Our embedder
inherits both: it steers only \emph{within} the reference block schedule, and it borrows the
frontier-anchored commit rule rather than inventing a schedule of its own.

\paragraph{Watermarks for autoregressive models.}
The dominant family biases token probabilities. \citet{kirchenbauer2023watermark} seed a
green/red vocabulary partition on the preceding token and add a logit bias; detection counts
green tokens against a binomial null, and the scheme's reliability and robustness have been
studied extensively~\citep{kirchenbauer2024reliability,zhao2023invisible,zhao2024provable}.
A second family avoids distortion by fixing the sampling randomness with a keyed
pseudorandom sequence, either through the Gumbel-max
trick~\citep{aaronson2023gpt,fu2024gumbelsoft} or with explicit distortion-freeness and
undetectability guarantees~\citep{kuditipudi2024distortionfree,christ2024undetectable};
others place the signature in model parameters rather than in
sampling~\citep{block2025gaussmark}. Surveys cover the
space~\citep{petitcolas1999hiding,liang2026survey}, and the threat model is
correspondingly well studied, from paraphrase attacks~\citep{krishna2023paraphrasing} to
extraction of the key itself~\citep{jovanovic2024stealing}. What every one of these has in
common is the \emph{verification} step: the detector computes a function of the token
identities.

\paragraph{Watermarks beyond left-to-right generation.}
Because the green list is seeded by a prefix that order-agnostic decoding does not provide,
transferring the autoregressive constructions to dLLMs requires care.
\citet{chen2025orderagnostic} give a watermark for order-agnostic models by biasing token
selection in a way that does not assume a prefix. For diffusion language models
specifically, \citet{gloaguen2026watermarking} apply a red--green bias \emph{in expectation}
over unresolved context and additionally prefer tokens that make other positions green;
\citet{bagchi2025discrete} use keyed Gumbel-max sampling at each denoising step while
preserving the sampling distribution; \citet{wu2025dmark} and \citet{raban2026lrdwm} target
the same setting with order-agnostic and efficiency-oriented constructions. Closest to our
embedding channel is dgMARK~\citep{hong2026dgmark}, which leaves the learned conditionals
untouched and instead prefers to unmask positions whose already-preferred token satisfies a
keyed parity condition; that discipline is precisely why it is inexpensive, and we adopt it.

\paragraph{What is new here.}
All of the above differ in \emph{where the watermark is placed} --- token probabilities,
sampling randomness, decoding order --- while agreeing on \emph{what the verifier reads}: a
function of the emitted symbols. ReTrace changes the second. The verifier masks part of the
finished text under the key, has the model reconstruct it, and compares the reconstructions;
the evidence is the model's response, which no token-level statistic can express. The price
is equally clear and we report it in every table: those detectors need no model calls, ours
needs a fixed number of sequence evaluations. To our knowledge no prior watermark for
language models --- autoregressive or diffusion --- uses the generator itself as the
verification oracle in this way.
