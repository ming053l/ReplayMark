\caption{\textbf{Where the watermark can be written.} The verifier's response bank replayed
over an unwatermarked 512-token LLaDA output. (a)~Admission score
$S_i=2\min(q_{i,+},q_{i,-})$ at every position of one document; orange outlines mark the
rejected positions ($S_i\leq s_{\min}=0.5$), where the block-masked conditional concentrates
on one response sign and no bit can be written. (b)~Across $1024$ positions of two documents,
$97\%$ pass the gate; since $q_{i,+}+q_{i,-}\approx 1$ on real text,
$S_i\approx 1-|q_{i,+}-q_{i,-}|$, and the gate removes the sharp tail. Thirty of the
thirty-one rejected positions sit within the first six tokens of a block, where the
conditional is most constrained. (c)~A strong carrier: the conditional spreads its mass over
tokens of both response signs, so either sign can be committed from a plausible token.
(d)~A one-sided position: a single dominant token fixes the response, and the position is
rejected before the key's orientation is consulted.}
